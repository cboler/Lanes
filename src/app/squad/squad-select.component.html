War Room: Squad Formation

Four mercenaries. Three lanes.

Deploy 1–4 units per side; a full squad of four is recommended. Melee units start in front, while ranged and support units start at the rear. The fourth unit shares Lane I.

Tactical triangle: Melee beats Ranged · Ranged beats Magic · Magic beats Melee. Specialists are neutral.
