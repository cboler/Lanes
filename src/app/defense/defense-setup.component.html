Defense Orders

Choose up to four roster mercenaries to defend, program their first four turns, then challenge them in a local practice battle.

Each member follows its own four-turn list. Stunned turns use a slot. An unavailable order falls back to role-based tactics. After turn four, all members use those tactics.

Target priority applies to single-target skills; area skills affect every legal target.
