# Thornwatch overworld artwork

`thornwatch.webp` is original generated background artwork for the Thornwatch expedition. It was created with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-21, then encoded as WebP (quality 88) for the game. Paths, waypoints, treasure markers, company and enemy pieces, animation, and the HUD are rendered separately by the application.

The reference for board-game presentation and turn-based traversal was the [Grand Kingdom review and screenshots on RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/4922-grand-kingdom-review). No screenshot assets from that article are distributed with the game.

## Generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background artwork for a playable fantasy tactical RPG overworld map, 1536x1024 landscape. Create an original beautifully hand-painted miniature diorama landscape seen from a high oblique overhead camera, like a detailed Japanese fantasy strategy board game. Rolling moss and olive green hills, little dark spruce forests around the outer edges, winding turquoise stream through the middle, stone bridge over river at center, weathered ruined stone tower and pale distant mountains on the right, small friendly fortified guild encampment on the left, golden late afternoon light, warm mist, exquisite fine painterly brush textures, muted woodland colors with golden highlights. Keep the broad central lower terrain relatively open and readable for gameplay paths that will be drawn in code. Compose for a wide game board. No people, no chess pieces, no route lines, no UI, no text, no logos, no frames, no lettering. The camera looks down at terrain throughout; no visible sky. Sophisticated illustrated game environment, atmospheric depth, rich detailed scenic corners.
